## Agriculture in Somalia

### Unlocking the Potential of Our Land

Somalia has a long agricultural heritage, diverse farming landscapes, and valuable agricultural resources. Areas surrounding the Juba and Shabelle rivers are particularly important for crop production.

Agriculture offers opportunities to increase local food production, strengthen rural livelihoods, develop agricultural businesses, and expand access to markets.

Realizing this potential requires continued attention to water management, agricultural knowledge, suitable technologies, market access, and responsible land use.

At Sooyaal Agri, we believe that agriculture can play an important role in shaping a more productive and prosperous future for Somalia.

## Why Agriculture Matters

### Growing More Than Crops. Growing Opportunities.

Agriculture has the potential to contribute to Somalia’s economic development far beyond the farm itself.

- **Food security:** Strengthening local food production and reducing vulnerability to supply disruptions.
- **Employment:** Creating opportunities throughout farming, transportation, processing, and trade.
- **Economic growth:** Supporting agricultural entrepreneurship and the development of local businesses.
- **Market opportunities:** Connecting agricultural production with buyers and broader markets.
- **Sustainability:** Encouraging responsible use of land and water for the benefit of future generations.

Developing agriculture is an investment in people, communities, and Somalia’s future.
